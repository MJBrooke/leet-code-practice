Webhooks are a 'push' mechanism that often replaces old polling techniques.
It allows a place like Adyen to proactively invoke a merchant's API to signal something or push data.
It sits at a well known pre-configured URL.

This allows for:
    - Pushing data on a change rather than polling
    - Building retry strategies into Adyen itself
    - Putting continuous failures into a DLQ

The merchant must make this webhook idempotent as it can be hit multiple times.
Merchant must track idempotency key of some kind, like the reference from Adyen.
Timestamps and sequence numbers are used to further disambiguate by the merchant.
An HMAC signature must always be included by Adyen for the merchant to verify.
